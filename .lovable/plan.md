# Remodel Wound Suturing Mini-Game

## Changes
- Replace the simple wound strip with a more natural skin incision using layered edges, depth, and subtle surrounding texture.
- Change each stitch target into an alternating left-to-right/right-to-left diagonal path, creating a clear zigzag suturing motion.
- Require each drag to begin at the highlighted entry side and finish at the opposite side while retaining the existing accuracy, timer, score, pause, and completion behavior.
- Rotate the play area through four skin tones—light, tan, brown, and dark—each time the mini-game starts.
- Keep guidance and completed sutures readable across every skin tone.

## Validation
- Check TypeScript validation.
- Launch the mini-game in the mobile layout and confirm the zigzag guidance, alternating drag direction, wound appearance, and readable controls.
