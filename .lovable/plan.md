# Nurses’ station visual redesign

## Scope
- Remove the instructional banner from the ward view.
- Redesign the bottom station as a compact curved/U-shaped pale clinical counter.
- Keep the current level title, but present it as a small integrated station nameplate rather than a separate instruction panel.
- Show five chairs along the station’s outer edge.
- Add a large pale-blue monitor at the centre of the desk and a small desk phone beside it.
- Keep spare desk surface visibly open for future upgrade items.
- Seat the active nurse behind the station when idle; hired staff sit in the other chairs while available and leave their chairs when dispatched.
- Simplify the top HUD to timer, ward health, points, and combo only.

## Visual details
- Preserve the current soft cartoon hospital palette, outlines, shadows, and compact mobile proportions.
- Build the counter and equipment with lightweight HTML/CSS shapes and familiar icons so they remain crisp at phone sizes.
- Keep all ward movement, staff automation, scoring, events, beds, and mini-games unchanged.

## Validation
- Check the ward at a mobile viewport.
- Confirm the station, five chairs, monitor, phone, seated nurse/staff, and simplified HUD are visible without obscuring beds.
- Confirm tapping the station still returns the nurse and staff can still dispatch normally.
